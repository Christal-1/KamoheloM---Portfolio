# Content Strategy

## Campaign content goal
Create useful, confidence-building content that leads the audience from curiosity to inquiry.

## Themes
- Why strategy matters for creative brands.
- The bridge between brand clarity and campaign performance.
- Real-world campaign ideas that feel intentional.

## Content pillars

### 1. Strategy education
Explain the value of a strategy-first approach to brand and campaign work.

### 2. Proof and process
Share how a clear marketing process can reduce uncertainty and improve results.

### 3. Conversion and action
Give audiences a clear next step with a low-friction offer or consultation.

## Content formats
- Short social posts and carousel stories.
- Mini case study snippets.
- Email sequence with tactical insight.
- Landing page narrative with a clear call to action.

## Content cadence
- Week 1: Awareness posts and value-led social content.
- Week 2: Education and process content to build trust.
- Week 3: Action-focused content with a clear consult offer.

## Key messages
- "Strategy makes marketing easier to understand." 
- "Your brand should be a guide, not a guessing game." 
- "A campaign that feels intentional is more likely to convert."

## Distribution plan
- Instagram for visual storytelling and inspiration.
- LinkedIn for authority-building insights.
- Email to deepen the relationship with interested leads.
- Landing page for a focused campaign destination.

## Measurement focus
- Engagement and saves as indicators of interest.
- Landing page traffic and form submissions.
- Email open and click rates.

## Recommended next step
Build a content calendar that maps each asset to a channel and campaign stage.
