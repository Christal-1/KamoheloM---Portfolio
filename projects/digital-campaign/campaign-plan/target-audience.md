# Target Audience

## Primary audience
Creative founders and founders of service-led lifestyle brands who want clearer marketing and better campaign outcomes.

### Demographic overview
- Age: 28–42
- Gender: all genders
- Business type: coaches, stylists, consultants, small lifestyle brands
- Location: urban and regional markets, especially South Africa and southern Africa

### Motivations
- Want marketing that feels aligned with their brand values.
- Want clarity on what will actually attract ideal clients.
- Need campaigns that support long-term business growth.

### Needs
- A clear brand story that resonates.
- A campaign that feels cohesive and purposeful.
- Practical guidance rather than buzzword-heavy recommendations.

### Pain points
- Confusing or inconsistent brand messaging.
- Campaigns with lots of activity but weak results.
- Difficulty choosing the best channels and content types.

### Preferred channels
- Instagram for visual inspiration.
- LinkedIn for professional credibility.
- Email for deeper conversation and follow-up.
- Website / landing page for decision-making.

## Audience segments

### Strategic founder
- Wants to build a business that stands out through clarity and expertise.
- Responds to thought leadership and process-driven content.

### Creative business owner
- Values strong visuals and a brand that feels premium.
- Needs proof that marketing decisions will support growth.

### Results-oriented founder
- Focused on improving conversion and performance.
- Looks for campaign ideas anchored in measurable outcomes.

## Engagement triggers
- "Show me how this will work for my business." 
- "Help me feel confident in my brand direction." 
- "I want a campaign that looks good and delivers results."

## Recommended messaging focus

Build content that shows how strategy creates clarity, why that clarity matters, and how campaign ideas can move a business forward.
