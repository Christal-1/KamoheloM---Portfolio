# Social Content Plan

## Campaign story
Position the brand as a strategic partner for founders who want brand clarity and campaign momentum.

## Content goals
- Build awareness through useful, strategic insights.
- Raise credibility with process and proof.
- Invite the audience to take a concrete next step.

## Content ideas
- Post 1: "Why strategy is the most powerful marketing tool you can use." 
- Post 2: "The three questions every founder should ask before launching a campaign." 
- Post 3: "How clarity in your brand message can save time and increase confidence." 
- Post 4: "A simple campaign framework for creative businesses." 
- Post 5: "What good marketing feels like: not noisy, but meaningful."

## Content formats
- Carousel posts summarising key marketing insights.
- Short video or reels with quick strategy tips.
- Quote cards with a clear takeaway.
- Behind-the-scenes process snippets.

## Channel focus
- Instagram: visual storytelling and saved content.
- LinkedIn: short-form thought leadership and personal insights.

## Posting schedule
- 2–3 posts per week in the campaign window.
- 1 story or short-form update mid-week.
- 1 email follow-up to engaged audiences after the campaign launches.

## Engagement hooks
- Ask a question at the end of each post.
- Include a clear next step, such as "Save this post" or "DM to learn more."
- Drive traffic to a landing page for the campaign offer.

## Creative direction
- Confident, simple visuals with bold headings.
- Calm, grounded colour palette and clean typography.
- Story-led captions that connect to the audience’s goals.

## Recommended next step
Create the first content batch and align the visuals with the campaign messaging framework.
