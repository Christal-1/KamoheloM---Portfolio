DIGITAL CAMPAIGN / 03

Content with
purpose.

Creating content that connects the campaign message with the people it is designed to reach.

01

The role of content

Content is one of the main ways a campaign communicates with its audience.

Effective content should have a clear role within the wider customer journey.

02

Content pillars

  • Educate
  • Inform
  • Engage
  • Build trust
  • Encourage action
03

Platform thinking

Different platforms require different ways of communicating.

The message should remain consistent while the format, tone and execution adapt to the platform and audience.

04

Content system

A structured content system creates consistency across the campaign.

It helps connect individual posts and pieces of content to the larger strategic objective.
