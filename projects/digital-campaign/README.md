# 02 — Digital Campaign: From Attention to Action

**Project type:** Mock campaign  
**Focus:** Digital marketing • Content strategy • Conversion

## Project overview

A multi-channel campaign concept built around the customer journey — from first awareness to meaningful action.

## Campaign objective

Design a campaign that moves a target audience through three stages:

1. Awareness
2. Consideration
3. Action

## Campaign framework

### Awareness
Build recognition through useful and relevant content.

Possible outputs:
- Short-form social content
- Awareness video concept
- Educational content

### Consideration
Give the audience a reason to trust and explore.

Possible outputs:
- Landing page concept
- Testimonials
- Comparison content
- Email sequence

### Action
Create a clear next step and reduce friction.

Possible outputs:
- Call-to-action
- Offer structure
- Conversion journey
- Retargeting concept

## Channel strategy

Each channel should have a specific role.

Possible channels:
- Instagram / TikTok
- LinkedIn
- Search
- Email
- Website / landing page

## Measurement

Track:

- Reach
- Engagement rate
- Click-through rate
- Landing page conversion
- Cost per acquisition
- Return on ad spend

## Suggested final deliverables

- Campaign brief
- Audience profile
- Customer journey
- Content calendar
- Social media mockups
- Campaign measurement plan
