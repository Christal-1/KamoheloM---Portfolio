# Campaign Metrics

## Campaign performance goals

Track the metrics that show whether the campaign is building interest, trust, and action.

## Core metrics

- Reach: the number of people who saw the campaign content.
- Engagement rate: likes, comments, saves, and shares relative to impressions.
- Click-through rate: percentage of people who clicked through from content to the landing page.
- Conversion rate: percent of visitors who completed the campaign action.
- Cost per acquisition (CPA): the average cost to acquire a lead or inquiry.
- Return on ad spend (ROAS): value generated versus media spend.

## Secondary metrics

- Website session duration.
- Bounce rate on campaign landing pages.
- Email open rate.
- Email click rate.
- Form submission rate.

## Measurement model

### Awareness stage
- Reach
- Impressions
- Engagement rate

### Consideration stage
- Content clicks
- Landing page traffic
- Time on page

### Action stage
- Conversion rate
- Inquiries generated
- CPA

## Reporting cadence

- Weekly summary of campaign performance.
- End-of-campaign review with lessons and recommendations.

## Insights to capture

- Which content types drove the most clicks.
- Which channels produced the highest engagement.
- Whether the landing page and email sequence supported the desired action.

## Recommended next step

Use these metrics to refine the campaign each week and focus on the channels producing the best results.
