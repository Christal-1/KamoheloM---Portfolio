02 — Digital Campaign

From Attention to Action

A multi-channel campaign concept built around the customer journey — from first awareness to meaningful action.

Mock Campaign Digital Marketing Content Strategy Conversion

01

The Campaign Objective

The objective was to design a digital campaign that moves a target audience through three key stages of the customer journey:

Awareness

Build recognition through useful, relevant and engaging content.

Consideration

Give the audience a reason to trust, explore and learn more.

Action

Create a clear next step and reduce friction in the conversion journey.

03

From Awareness to Action

Each stage of the campaign has a specific role in moving the audience closer to meaningful action.

Awareness

Short-form social content, educational content and awareness concepts introduce the audience to the campaign.

Consideration

Landing pages, testimonials, comparison content and email nurture help build trust.

Action

Clear calls-to-action, offers and a simplified conversion journey encourage the next step.

Retargeting

Retargeting concepts reconnect with people who interacted with the campaign but did not convert.

04

Channel Strategy

Each channel is assigned a specific role within the campaign rather than simply distributing the same content everywhere.

01

Social Media

Build awareness and engagement through useful, relevant and platform-specific content.

02

Search

Capture high-intent audiences actively looking for solutions.

03

Email & Website

Support consideration and guide the audience toward conversion.

05

Measurement

Campaign performance would be measured across the full customer journey.

Reach

How many people were exposed to the campaign?

Engagement

How effectively did the content capture attention?

Click-through Rate

How many people took the next step?

Conversion

How effectively did the campaign drive action?

06

Expected Outcome

The campaign demonstrates how a connected customer journey can turn attention into consideration and consideration into action.

The key strategic principle is that every channel and piece of content should have a clear role in moving the audience forward.
