# Data Dictionary

## Overview
This data dictionary describes the key marketing metrics and dimensions used in the marketing analytics project.

## Metrics

### Impressions
The total number of times marketing content was displayed to the audience.

### Reach
The number of unique people who saw the campaign content.

### Engagements
The total number of interactions such as likes, comments, shares, and saves.

### Engagement rate
Engagements divided by impressions, expressed as a percentage.

### Click-through rate (CTR)
The share of people who clicked from marketing content to the landing page.

### Conversion rate
The percentage of landing page visitors who completed a desired action.

### Cost per acquisition (CPA)
The average cost to acquire a customer or qualified lead.

### Return on ad spend (ROAS)
Revenue generated divided by advertising spend.

### Sessions
Number of visits to the website or campaign landing page.

### Bounce rate
The percentage of visitors who leave after viewing only one page.

## Dimensions

### Channel
The marketing channel, such as Instagram, LinkedIn, email, or search.

### Campaign stage
The stage of the customer journey: awareness, consideration, or action.

### Audience segment
A group defined by shared characteristics, such as founder type or business need.

### Content type
The format of the asset, such as social post, email, landing page, or case study.

## Notes
Consistent tracking of campaign stages, channel definitions, and content categories helps ensure the analytics are easy to compare and analyze.
