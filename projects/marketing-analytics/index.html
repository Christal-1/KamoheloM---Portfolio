03 — Marketing Intelligence

Data That Moves

A marketing intelligence project exploring how campaign data can become insight, and how insight can become the next strategic decision.

Analytics Concept Marketing Analytics KPIs Business Insight

01

The Business Question

Which channels and customer segments are contributing most effectively to campaign performance?

This project explores how marketing data can be transformed into clear insights that support better business decisions.

02

The Data

The analysis brings together different marketing data sources to create a broader view of campaign performance.

Campaign Performance

Understanding how campaigns perform across channels and audiences.

Website Traffic

Analysing traffic sources and how users move through the conversion journey.

Customer Segments

Comparing the behaviour and performance of different customer groups.

03

Key Performance Indicators

The analysis focuses on metrics that connect marketing activity to business performance.

Engagement

Impressions, reach and engagement rate help measure audience attention.

Acquisition

Click-through rate and cost per acquisition help evaluate efficiency.

Conversion

Conversion rate helps measure how effectively traffic becomes action.

Return

Return on ad spend connects marketing investment to financial performance.

04

From Data to Insight

The analysis looks for patterns that reveal where performance is strongest, where opportunities exist and where optimisation may be needed.

Channel Performance

Identify the channels delivering the strongest results and compare performance efficiency.

Audience Segments

Identify the customer groups contributing most effectively to campaign outcomes.

Conversion Drop-off

Identify where potential customers leave the journey and where improvements may be needed.

05

Dashboard Direction

The dashboard is designed to bring the most important performance information together in one place.

01

Marketing Performance Dashboard

A dashboard concept bringing together KPIs, channel performance, conversion behaviour and marketing return.

06

Strategic Recommendation

Based on the performance data, budget should be reallocated toward channels generating the strongest conversion efficiency while improving weaker stages of the customer journey.

The core principle is simple:

Data should not only explain what happened. It should help determine what happens next.
