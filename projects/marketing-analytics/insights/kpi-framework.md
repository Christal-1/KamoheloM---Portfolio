# KPI Framework

## Purpose
Define the key performance indicators used to evaluate campaign health and marketing effectiveness.

## Primary KPIs

### Reach
The number of unique people exposed to campaign content.

### Engagement rate
A measure of how the audience interacts with content relative to impressions.

### Click-through rate (CTR)
The share of content viewers who click through to the campaign landing page.

### Conversion rate
The percentage of landing page visitors who take the desired action.

### Cost per acquisition (CPA)
The average spend required to acquire a qualified lead or customer.

### Return on ad spend (ROAS)
Revenue or value generated per unit of ad spend.

## Supporting KPIs

### Bounce rate
The percentage of visitors leaving the landing page after a single page view.

### Time on page
Average time visitors spend on the landing page.

### Email open rate
The percentage of recipients who open a campaign email.

### Email click rate
The percentage of recipients who click a link inside an email.

## KPI categories

### Awareness
- Reach
- Impressions
- Engagement rate

### Consideration
- CTR
- Time on page
- Email engagement

### Action
- Conversion rate
- CPA
- ROAS

## KPI targets

- Engagement rate above industry baseline.
- CTR strong enough to sustain audience interest.
- Conversion rate high enough to generate meaningful inquiry volume.
- CPA and ROAS showing efficient use of campaign resources.

## Notes
Use this framework to monitor performance, compare channel outcomes, and refine campaign decisions.
