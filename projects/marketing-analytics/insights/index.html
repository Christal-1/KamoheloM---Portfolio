MARKETING ANALYTICS / 03

Finding the
story in the data.

Turning marketing performance data into insights that can support better strategic decisions.

01

Data is not the insight

Numbers describe what happened.

Insight goes further by asking why something happened, what it means and what should happen next.

02

Insight framework

  • What happened?
  • Why did it happen?
  • What does it mean?
  • What opportunity exists?
  • What should happen next?
03

Connecting insight to strategy

The value of analysis is realised when insight influences a decision.

Marketing insights can inform targeting, messaging, content, budget allocation and future campaign activity.

04

The opportunity

Marketing analytics creates a bridge between creative activity and measurable business outcomes.

The goal is to make better decisions by understanding the evidence behind them.
