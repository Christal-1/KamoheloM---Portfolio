Market
Research.

Understanding the market, the audience and the competitive environment before making strategic decisions.

01

Why research matters

Strong brand strategy begins with understanding. Before developing a position or campaign, it is important to understand the environment in which a brand operates.

This research explores the market, audience and competitive landscape to identify patterns, needs and opportunities.

02

Research focus

  • Market environment
  • Target audience
  • Customer needs and behaviours
  • Competitor positioning
  • Market opportunities
03

Key questions

The research is structured around several strategic questions:

  • Who is the customer?
  • What does the customer need?
  • What influences their decisions?
  • How are competitors positioned?
  • Where are the gaps and opportunities?
04

Research output

The goal is to move beyond collecting information. Research should create a clear foundation for strategic decisions.

The findings from this stage inform the audience profile, competitive analysis, brand positioning and messaging framework.
