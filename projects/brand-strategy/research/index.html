BRAND STRATEGY / RESEARCH

Understanding the Opportunity

Research provides the foundation for strategic decision-making by revealing the market context, competitive landscape and audience needs.

Market Research Competitor Analysis Audience Insight

01

Market Research

Market research helps establish an understanding of the broader category, the environment in which the brand operates and the opportunities that may exist.

Category Context

Understanding the market, category trends and the factors influencing customer decisions.

Market Opportunity

Identifying gaps, unmet needs and opportunities for the brand to create greater relevance.

02

Competitor Analysis

Understanding competitors helps identify how brands currently communicate, where they compete and where opportunities for differentiation may exist.

01

Competitive Positioning

Comparing how competing brands position themselves within the category.

02

Communication Gaps

Identifying areas where existing communication may not fully address audience needs.

03

Differentiation Opportunities

Exploring potential ways for the brand to establish a more distinctive position.

03

Audience Profile

Understanding the audience helps move the strategy beyond assumptions and toward a clearer understanding of what people need, value and respond to.

Needs

What problems is the audience trying to solve?

Motivations

What influences the audience's decisions and behaviour?

Barriers

What prevents the audience from taking action?

Behaviours

How does the audience discover, evaluate and choose products or services?

04

Research Outcome

The research creates a foundation for identifying a clearer strategic opportunity and developing a brand position that is more relevant to the target audience.

These insights inform the next stage of the project: brand strategy and positioning.
