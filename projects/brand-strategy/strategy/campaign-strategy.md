# Campaign Strategy

## Campaign goal

Build awareness and trust for a strategy-led brand service, while generating qualified leads from ambitious creative founders.

## Campaign objective

- Increase brand recall among target audiences.
- Position the brand as the go-to partner for strategy-driven marketing.
- Generate audience interest through clear, outcome-focused content.

## Strategic approach

1. Educate the audience on why strategy matters.
2. Show the link between brand clarity and campaign performance.
3. Offer an easy next step for founders who want a stronger marketing direction.

## Campaign pillars

- Insight-led storytelling
- Practical campaign proof
- Clear conversion path

## Key message themes

- Why a deeper strategy is the best starting point.
- How clarity in brand messaging reduces wasted marketing effort.
- What success looks like for a founder who chooses a strategic partner.

## Channel mix

- Instagram: visual stories, client outcomes, campaign concepts.
- LinkedIn: thought leadership, process explainers, case study snippets.
- Email: nurture sequence, campaign reflections, offer invitations.
- Landing page: campaign narrative, service offer, contact action.

## Campaign stages

### Awareness
- Share short stories and posts that explain the value of strategy.
- Use carousel or short video formats that highlight before/after thinking.

### Consideration
- Publish a mini case study or campaign framework that shows the process.
- Create an educational guide or checklist for founders.

### Action
- Offer a low-friction consultation or strategy review.
- Use a focused landing page with a clear call to action.

## Success metrics

- Social engagement and saves.
- Landing page visits and time on page.
- Inquiry requests from ideal audience members.
- Email open and click-through rates.

## Tactical concept

A campaign themed around "Strategy that feels simple and works" with content that contrasts common marketing clutter against a clearer, outcome-led alternative.

## Recommended next step

Develop creative content assets and a social plan that bring the campaign story to life while keeping the message consistent across channels.
