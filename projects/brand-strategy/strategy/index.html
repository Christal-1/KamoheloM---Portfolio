BRAND STRATEGY / STRATEGY

Building a Sharper Position

Translating research and audience understanding into a clear brand position, messaging framework and campaign direction.

Brand Positioning Messaging Campaign Strategy

01

Brand Positioning

Brand positioning defines the space a brand aims to own in the mind of its audience.

Audience

A clearly defined audience with specific needs, motivations and expectations.

Category

A clear understanding of the market and competitive environment.

Difference

A meaningful point of difference that gives the audience a reason to choose the brand.

Position

A clear and relevant space the brand can own in the customer's mind.

02

Messaging Framework

The messaging framework translates the strategic position into clear communication that can be used consistently across different channels.

01

Core Message

The central idea the brand wants the audience to understand and remember.

02

Supporting Messages

Key messages that reinforce the brand's value and strategic position.

03

Proof Points

Evidence and reasons that support the brand's claims and build credibility.

03

Campaign Strategy

The campaign strategy connects the brand position to a communication direction designed to reach and engage the target audience.

Objective

Define what the campaign needs to achieve.

Audience

Identify who the campaign needs to reach and influence.

Campaign Idea

Develop a central idea that connects the audience insight to the brand strategy.

Communication

Translate the campaign idea into relevant content and channel activity.

04

Strategic Outcome

The strategy creates a clear connection between audience understanding, brand positioning and communication.

The result is a strategic foundation that can guide future messaging, campaigns and marketing activity.
