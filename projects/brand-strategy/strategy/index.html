BRAND STRATEGY / 02

Strategy that
creates direction.

Turning research and customer understanding into a clear strategic direction for the brand.

01

From insight to strategy

Research only becomes valuable when it informs a decision. The strategy stage transforms customer and market insight into a clear direction for the brand.

This involves identifying the most meaningful opportunity and defining how the brand should respond.

02

Strategic focus

  • Brand positioning
  • Customer value proposition
  • Brand differentiation
  • Strategic opportunity
  • Brand direction
03

The strategic challenge

A brand needs to be understood by the people it is trying to reach.

The challenge is therefore to create a position that is both meaningful to the customer and distinctive within the market.

04

Strategic outcome

The outcome is a focused strategic foundation that guides the brand's communication, messaging and future marketing activity.

Strategy creates the direction. The next step is translating that direction into a clear brand message.
