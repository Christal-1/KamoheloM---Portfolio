# 01 — Brand Strategy: Repositioning a Brand

**Project type:** Mock portfolio case study  
**Focus:** Customer insight • Brand positioning • Strategy

## Project overview

A strategic exploration of how customer understanding can create a sharper brand position and a stronger connection with a new audience.

This project is designed to demonstrate strategic thinking rather than simply showing a final design.

## The challenge

How can a brand with a strong product become more relevant to a specific audience?

## Project process

### 1. Research
Explore the market, category and audience.

Suggested outputs:
- Competitor scan
- Customer observations
- Social listening notes
- Audience pain points
- Market opportunity summary

Place research materials in:

`research/`

### 2. Audience
Define the audience the brand is trying to reach.

Suggested outputs:
- Audience profile
- Customer needs
- Motivations
- Barriers
- Customer journey

### 3. Positioning
Identify what the brand should own in the mind of the customer.

Suggested outputs:
- Positioning statement
- Value proposition
- Competitor positioning map
- Key differentiator

Place strategy work in:

`strategy/`

### 4. Strategic direction
Translate insight into a clear direction for the brand.

Suggested outputs:
- Brand message
- Campaign territory
- Tone of voice
- Content pillars

### 5. Visual direction
Create a visual direction that supports the strategy.

Suggested outputs:
- Moodboard
- Typography direction
- Colour direction
- Sample social post

Place visual work in:

`visuals/`

## Final outcome

The final case study should clearly show:

**Problem → Insight → Strategy → Direction → Expected outcome**

- `positioning-map.png`
