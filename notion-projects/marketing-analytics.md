# Marketing Analytics — Data that moves

## Overview

A marketing analytics project that turns campaign performance data into clear insight and strategic recommendations.

## Challenge

Data existed, but it was not organised or framed for decision-making. The goal was to create a practical analytics structure that supports better marketing decisions.

## Approach

- Documented key metrics, dimensions and definitions in a data dictionary.
- Proposed dashboard views that highlight channel performance, audience behaviour, and funnel conversion.
- Built an insights framework focused on the highest impact actions.
- Created a KPI model that ties campaign results to business outcomes.

## Deliverables

- Data dictionary
- Dashboard plan
- Marketing insights
- KPI framework

## Outcome

A structured analytics foundation that helps the team understand which campaign activities matter, why they matter, and what to do next.

## Reflection

This project shows how disciplined analytics turns marketing activity into meaningful growth guidance, not just reports and metrics.
