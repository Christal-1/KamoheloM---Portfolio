# Digital Campaign — From attention to action

## Overview

A campaign strategy project designed to move audiences from awareness through consideration and into meaningful action.

## Challenge

The brand needed a campaign plan that felt purposeful and measurable, not just visually attractive. The objective was to create a journey that turns interest into leads or inquiries.

## Approach

- Defined campaign stages aligned to audience mindset: awareness, interest, action.
- Identified the content and channels that would best support each stage.
- Created headline messaging that connects with founders who need clarity, trust, and momentum.
- Built a measurement model to track reach, engagement, conversion and campaign ROI.

## Deliverables

- Campaign brief
- Target audience profile
- Content strategy
- Social content plan
- Campaign measurement framework

## Outcome

A campaign concept that balances brand positioning with practical digital execution, making it easier to communicate strategy and evaluate performance.

## Reflection

This project illustrates how marketing campaigns can be grounded in audience insight and business objectives, not just design or trend-based ideas.
